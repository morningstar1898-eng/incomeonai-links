Part 1: The H.A.V.E. Framework

Every viral faceless video follows this structure. Not some. Every.

The framework is simple: Hook, Agitate, Value, Exit-CTA. Master each piece, fill in the blanks, and you'll produce scroll-stopping videos in under 10 minutes.

H — Hook (0-3 seconds)

The first sentence your viewer hears. This is the ONLY thing that determines whether they watch or scroll. Everything else is irrelevant if the hook fails.

Formula: [Shocking result/number] + [Relatable context] + [Implied secret]

Examples:

  • "I made $6K/month posting AI videos I never appeared in"
  • "This free AI tool does in 5 minutes what editors charge $200 for"
  • "3 apps. 8 minutes. That's what a faceless YouTube channel actually takes"
Fill in yours: "I [RESULT] by [METHOD] — and [UNEXPECTED TWIST]"

A — Agitate (3-10 seconds)

Make the viewer feel the problem. They KNOW content creation is hard. Validate that, then pivot.

Formula: [Common belief] + "But here's what nobody tells you" + [Reframe]

Examples:

  • "Everyone says you need a $3K camera and ring light to start on YouTube. Nobody mentions the creators making $5K/month with a $0 tool stack and no face on camera."
  • "You've been told content creation takes hours a day. The truth? The top faceless creators spend 60 minutes — total — and post 3x more than you."
Fill in yours: "Everyone says [COMMON BELIEF]. But [REFRAME THAT OPENS A LOOP]."

V — Value (10-50 seconds)

3 punchy points. Each one is: tool/method name + what it does + the result.

Per point: "First: [TOOL/METHOD]. It [WHAT IT DOES]. That means [RESULT IN HUMAN TERMS]."

Rules:

  • Each point is 2 sentences MAX
  • Name specific tools (InVideo AI, not "a video tool")
  • Include at least one number per point
  • Transition words: "First... Second... And the game-changer..."

E — Exit-CTA (last 5-10 seconds)

ONE action. Not two. Not "follow and check out my link and subscribe." ONE.

Formula: "If you want [SPECIFIC OUTCOME], [SINGLE ACTION]. Link in bio."

Examples:

  • "If you want the full tool stack breakdown, grab the free guide. Link in bio."
  • "If you want the exact prompts I use, they're in the link in bio."
  • "Want me to build this for you? Book a free call. Link in bio."
Fill in yours: "If you want [OUTCOME], [ACTION]. Link in bio."

Part 3: The 3-Second Rule Checklist

Your video's first 3 seconds must contain ALL 7 of these elements. Score yourself honestly — 5/7 has a chance, 6/7 will perform, 7/7 is a potential viral hit.

  • Pattern interrupt — First word is unexpected (a number, "Stop," "Delete," "I")
  • Specificity — Contains at least one specific number ($6K, 3 tools, 60 seconds)
  • Implied result — Viewer can picture the outcome before you explain it
  • Curiosity gap — Something is left unsaid that makes them NEED to keep watching
  • Visual movement — Screen changes (text appears, zoom, transition) within first 2 seconds
  • Caption on screen — The hook text appears as a caption simultaneously with the voiceover
  • No preamble — Zero "hey guys" or "in this video." First word IS the hook.
