5 AI Prompts That Save Analysts 10+ Hours a Week

Copy these, swap in your details, and stop doing by hand what AI does in seconds. The rule: AI drafts, you verify. You still own the judgment — you just skip the grunt work. AI won't replace analysts. Analysts who use AI will replace the ones who don't.

1. The SQL Writer

Saves ~3–5 hrs/week
Act as a senior data analyst. Write a [BigQuery/Postgres/T-SQL] query that [goal]. Tables & key columns: [paste schema]. Explain the joins in one line each, and flag any assumptions you made.

Pro tip: Give it the schema first. 80% of "wrong" AI SQL is just the model guessing your columns. Then QA the joins before you trust the numbers.

2. The Formula Genie (Excel / Sheets)

Saves ~2 hrs/week
Write an Excel formula to [goal]. My columns: [list them, e.g. A=Date, B=Region, C=Revenue]. Prefer XLOOKUP/SUMIFS over nested IFs. Explain it in one sentence.

Pro tip: Ask it to "explain in one sentence" so you can defend the formula in a review — and learn it for next time.

3. The Data Cleaner

Saves ~3 hrs/week
Here is messy data: [paste]. Standardize dates to YYYY-MM-DD, fix inconsistent casing, trim whitespace, flag duplicates and obvious outliers. Return the cleaned data PLUS a bullet list of exactly what you changed.

Pro tip: Always ask for "what you changed." That list is your audit trail — never ship cleaned data you can't explain. Strip identifiers first if it's sensitive.

4. The Exec Summary

Saves ~2 hrs/week
Turn this analysis into a 5-bullet executive summary for non-technical leadership: [paste]. Lead with business impact ($ / time / risk), not methodology. End with one recommended action.

Pro tip: This is the skill that gets analysts promoted. Let AI draft the narrative; you supply the judgment on what actually matters.

5. The Dashboard Planner

Saves ~2 hrs/week
I have this dataset: [describe columns + what the business cares about]. Recommend the 4 most decision-useful charts, what question each answers, and the best chart type for each. No vanity metrics.

Pro tip: Plan the dashboard before you build it. 30 seconds here saves an afternoon of reworking visuals nobody uses.
