Lesson 1.1

Picking a Niche That Has Money In It

"Follow your passion" is advice from people who already have money. Your niche needs to pass three tests before you commit:

The 3-Criteria Test

  1. Audience size: Can you find at least 50 pieces of content in this niche that have 100K+ views? If yes, there's demand. Search TikTok and YouTube for your niche keyword. Sort by most viewed. Count.
  2. Monetization paths: Can you name at least 3 ways to make money? (Affiliate products, digital products, sponsorships, ad revenue, services.) If you can only think of one, the niche is too narrow.
  3. AI-ability: Can AI produce good content in this niche? Test it: give Claude your niche topic and ask for 5 video ideas with hooks. If the output is generic garbage, the niche requires too much personal expertise for a faceless brand.

Niches that pass all 3:

  • AI tools and automation
  • Personal finance / investing basics
  • Side hustles / making money online
  • Productivity and self-improvement
  • Tech reviews and tutorials
  • Health/fitness tips (evidence-based, not medical advice)
  • Career development / remote work

Niches that fail: Anything requiring certification (legal, medical), anything hyper-local (real estate in one city), anything with zero product ecosystem (niche hobbies with no tools to sell).

Decision time: Pick ONE niche. Not two. Not a "blend." You can expand later, but starting broad kills your algorithm performance. The algorithm rewards accounts that are about one thing.

The Content Flywheel: 1 Idea → 9+ Pieces

Most creators think one idea = one piece of content. That's why they burn out. The flywheel model means every idea produces at minimum 9 assets:

  1. Start with one topic (e.g., "3 AI tools that replace freelancers")

  6. LinkedIn post — Professional angle on the same insight

  8. Instagram carousel — 10-slide visual breakdown

Lesson 2.3

Video Scripting with the H.A.V.E. Framework

Every viral faceless video follows four beats: Hook, Agitate, Value, Exit-CTA.

Hook (0-3 seconds) — The first sentence determines everything. Formula: [Shocking result/number] + [Relatable context] + [Implied secret].

  • "I made $6K/month posting AI videos I never appeared in"
  • "This free AI tool does in 5 minutes what editors charge $200 for"

Agitate (3-10 seconds) — Validate the viewer's problem, then pivot. Formula: [Common belief] + "But here's what nobody tells you" + [Reframe].

Value (10-50 seconds) — 3 specific, actionable points. Each one: tool/method name + what it does + the result. Include one number per point.

Exit-CTA (last 5-10 seconds) — ONE action. Not "follow AND check the link AND subscribe." ONE. Formula: "If you want [specific outcome], [single action]. Link in bio."

The 3-Second Rule: Your video's first 3 seconds must contain: a pattern interrupt, a specific number, an implied result, a curiosity gap, visual movement, caption on screen, and zero preamble. Score yourself — 7/7 is a potential viral hit.
Lesson 2.4

Producing Faceless Videos in Under 10 Minutes

Here's the exact production workflow:

  1. Generate script (2 min) — Use the Viral Video Script Engine prompt with your topic and platform.
  2. Generate voiceover (1 min) — Paste the script into ElevenLabs. Select your consistent voice. Download the MP3.
  3. Generate video (3 min) — Upload to Creatomate or InVideo AI. Select a template (9:16 vertical). Add voiceover as audio track. The tool auto-generates visuals, text overlays, and transitions.
  4. Review and adjust (2 min) — Watch the output. Trim any dead space. Check that captions match audio. Make sure the hook text appears in the first 2 seconds.
  5. Schedule (1 min) — Upload to Buffer. Set publish time. Add caption with hashtags.

Total: 9 minutes per video. Do 3 per day = 27 minutes of production for content that runs on 3 platforms.

Lesson 3.2

The 30-Day Launch Strategy

Week 1 (Days 1-7): Post 1 video per day on all 3 platforms. Same video, adapted caption. Goal: establish presence, test what the algorithm shows.

Week 2 (Days 8-14): Increase to 2 videos per day. Start tracking which topics get more views. Double down on winners. Goal: find your content-market fit.

Week 3 (Days 15-21): 2-3 videos per day. Start engaging with comments on similar creators' posts. Reply to every comment on your content within 1 hour. Goal: first 100-500 followers.

Week 4 (Days 22-30): Maintain 2-3 videos per day. Add your lead magnet to link-in-bio. Start mentioning "free guide in bio" as CTA. Goal: first newsletter subscribers.

Reality check: Most accounts see minimal growth in the first 2 weeks. This is normal. The algorithm needs data on your content. Keep posting. The compound effect kicks in around day 15-20.
Lesson 3.3

Cross-Posting Strategy: 3x Reach Without 3x Work

Post the same core video to all 3 platforms but adapt the caption:

  • TikTok caption: Short, punchy, 2-3 hashtags. End with a question to drive comments.
  • YouTube Shorts: Longer description with keywords. First 2 lines matter most (shown before "...more"). Include #Shorts.
  • Instagram Reels: Longer caption with value. 15-20 hashtags in a comment (not the caption). End with "Save this for later."

Timing: Don't post at the same time. Stagger by 2-3 hours. This prevents the platforms from detecting cross-posted content and suppressing it.

Lesson 3.4

The Comment Strategy That Converts

Engagement isn't just replying to your own comments. It's strategically appearing in other people's conversations:

  1. Find 10 creators in your niche with 10K-100K followers (not millions — their comment sections are too crowded).
  2. Leave thoughtful comments on their posts within the first hour of posting. Not "Great post!" — add a genuine insight or personal experience.
  3. Reply to other commenters on those posts. Help people in the comments. This is where you get profile visits.
  4. Reply to every comment on your own posts within 1 hour. The algorithm boosts posts with active comment sections.

Time investment: 15 minutes/day. The ROI compounds — people who see your helpful comments visit your profile, see your content, and follow.

Lesson 3.5

SEO and Hashtag Strategy Per Platform

TikTok: Use 3-5 hashtags. Mix broad (#ai, #sidehustle) with specific (#aitoolsformoney, #facelesscontent). TikTok's search is becoming a discovery engine — keywords in your caption matter now.

YouTube: Title and description are SEO. Use keywords people actually search. Check YouTube search autocomplete — type your topic and see what it suggests. Those suggestions = real search volume.

Instagram: 15-20 hashtags in first comment. Organize in tiers: 5 large (#entrepreneur, 1M+ posts), 5 medium (#aicontent, 100K-1M posts), 5 small (#facelessbrand, under 100K posts). The small hashtags are where you can rank.
