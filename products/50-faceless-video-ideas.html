How to Pick Your Niche

Don't overthink this. Use these four questions to find your best fit in under 5 minutes:

1. Can you talk about this for 100 videos?

You don't need to be an expert. You just need to stay interested long enough to build momentum. If you'd get bored after 10 videos, move on.

2. Are people already spending money in this niche?

Search TikTok Shop, Amazon bestsellers, or Gumroad for your topic. If people are buying courses, tools, or templates, there's money on the table.

3. Can you make it faceless?

Some niches work better faceless than others. Screen recordings, AI voiceovers, stock footage, and text-on-screen all work great. If your niche needs you on camera to build trust (like fitness coaching), it's harder to go faceless.

4. Is there a clear product to sell?

The real money isn't in views. It's in what you sell to those viewers. Pick a niche where you can offer a digital product, affiliate link, or service. Every niche below has at least 3 ways to make money.

Pro tip: Start with ONE niche. Post 30 videos. Then look at your analytics. Your audience will tell you what they want more of. Don't try to cover all 10 niches at once.

How to Turn Any Idea Into a Script in 5 Minutes

You have 50 ideas now. But an idea without a script is just a thought. Here's how to go from idea to finished script in 5 minutes flat using AI tools.

Step 1: Paste This Prompt Into ChatGPT or Claude (30 seconds)

PROMPT: Write a 60-second faceless video script about [YOUR TOPIC]. Structure: - Hook (first 3 seconds): Start with a surprising fact or bold claim - Problem (next 5 seconds): Why this matters to the viewer - 3 main points (30 seconds): Specific, actionable tips with examples - CTA (last 5 seconds): One clear action Rules: Write at a 6th-grade reading level. Use short sentences. No filler words. Make it sound like a real person talking, not a textbook.

Step 2: Clean It Up (90 seconds)

Read the script out loud. If you stumble on any sentence, shorten it. If any point feels vague, add a number or a specific tool name. Cut anything that sounds like filler.

Rule of thumb: A 60-second video is about 150 words. If your script is over 160, cut something.

Step 3: Generate the Voiceover (60 seconds)

Use a free AI voice tool like ElevenLabs or the built-in voice feature in CapCut. Paste your script, pick a voice, and export.

Best voices for faceless content: Calm, confident, slightly fast-paced. Avoid overly robotic voices.

Step 4: Add Visuals (2 minutes)

Use screen recordings, stock footage from Pexels, or AI-generated images. Match the visual to each point in your script. Add captions (CapCut does this automatically).

Step 5: Export and Post (30 seconds)

Export at 1080x1920 (vertical). Post to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels at the same time. Same video, three platforms, three chances to go viral.

Speed hack: Batch your content. Pick 5 ideas from this list, write all 5 scripts in one sitting, film them all back-to-back, then schedule them across the week. One hour of work = one week of content.
